About Wander

Helping travellers see the world since 2015

Our Story

Wander started with a simple idea: international travel shouldn't be complicated or overpriced. What began as a small blog sharing budget travel tips has grown into a full travel platform, helping thousands of people book trips to over 40 countries every year. We partner directly with local guides so more of your money supports the communities you visit.
